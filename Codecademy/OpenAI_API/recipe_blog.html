Gluten-Free Keto Tuna Lettuce Wraps

Enjoy a delicious and healthy meal with these gluten-free and keto-friendly tuna lettuce wraps. Perfect for a quick lunch or dinner, this recipe combines fresh ingredients with a hint of flavor from Lizano sauce.

Ingredients

  • 1 can of tuna
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 1 tomato, diced
  • 1 tablespoon Lizano sauce
  • Salt to taste
  • Lettuce leaves (for wrapping)

Instructions

  1. In a skillet, heat the oil over medium heat and sauté the diced onion until translucent.
  2. Add the diced tomato and cook for another 2-3 minutes until softened.
  3. Stir in the canned tuna and Lizano sauce, mixing well. Season with salt to taste.
  4. Remove from heat and let the mixture cool slightly.
  5. Take a lettuce leaf and spoon the tuna mixture into the center, wrapping it up like a taco.
  6. Serve immediately and enjoy your healthy, gluten-free, and keto-friendly meal!
