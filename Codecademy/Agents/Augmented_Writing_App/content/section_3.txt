Section 3: HR Personnel Role Descriptions

This section outlines the key roles within the Human Resources (HR) department, their core functions, and responsibilities. Understanding these roles helps employees know whom to approach for different HR-related matters and clarifies the services provided by HR.

---

1. HR Director
- Responsible for overall HR strategy, compliance, and policy development.
- Oversees all HR operations including hiring, benefits administration, employee relations, and organizational development.
- Acts as the liaison between executive management and the HR department.
- Approves major HR initiatives and leads departmental projects.

2. HR Manager
- Manages the day-to-day HR team operations.
- Coordinates recruitment, onboarding, training, and performance management.
- Ensures HR policies are consistently applied across departments.
- Advises supervisors and employees on company policies, conflict resolution, and HR best practices.

3. HR Generalist
- Handles a broad range of HR tasks including recruiting, onboarding, benefits administration, and employee records.
- Assists with employee engagement and wellness programs.
- Provides support to employees with HR queries and concerns.

4. HR Coordinator
- Provides administrative support to the HR department.
- Schedules interviews, orientations, and training sessions.
- Maintains HR files and assists with reporting and documentation.
- Supports the recruitment process and organizes employee events.

5. Payroll & Benefits Specialist
- Manages payroll processing and ensures employees are paid accurately and on time.
- Administers employee benefits programs (healthcare, retirement plans, leave policies, etc.).
- Answers employees’ payroll and benefit questions.
- Maintains compliance with payroll regulations and benefits laws.

6. Talent Acquisition Specialist
- Focuses on attracting and hiring qualified talent for open positions.
- Develops job postings, screens candidates, coordinates interviews, and manages the offer process.
- Partners with hiring managers to understand staffing needs.

---

For any HR-related questions or assistance, employees are encouraged to contact the HR team. The HR department is committed to supporting staff needs, fostering a positive workplace environment, and ensuring compliance with company policies and employment laws.
